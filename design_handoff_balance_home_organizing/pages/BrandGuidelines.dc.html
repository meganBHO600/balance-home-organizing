Brand Guidelines

Balance Home Organizing

Pulled directly from balancehomeorganizing.com — the live site's own logo, palette, type and photography style, documented for consistent reuse.

Logo

A layered square monogram — three offset outlined squares behind a serif “B” — set in the ink color below on a white ground. Keep clear space around it equal to the mark's own width; never recolor the squares or fill them solid.

Color

Emerald (Primary)

#1F6361

Logo, buttons, links, headings

Sage (Secondary)

#9AAB89

Accents, tags, secondary marks

Salmon (Tertiary)

#E0A899

Soft highlights, tinted backgrounds

Ink

#26332F

Headings, body text

White

#FFFFFF

Page background

Emerald, sage and salmon are the three brand colors from the logo mark; ink and white anchor the page.

Type

Poppins, set throughout — headings at 600 weight, body copy at 400. Sizes below are measured directly off the live site's rendered pages.

H1 · 48px / 600 Is your home clutter causing you stress?
H2 · 28px / 600 Our Process
Body · 16px / 400 We are a team of professional organizers, located in San Diego, dedicated to bringing balance and simplicity in your home.
Nav link · 15px / 500 Services
Button label · 13px / 600, uppercase Book Consultation

Imagery

Real client spaces, shot naturally lit and true-to-color. Service and process photography sits in a full circle crop; hero and section imagery runs full-bleed and rectangular, never boxed or matted.

Voice

Warm, encouraging, plain-spoken. Short sentences, first person plural (“we”), no jargon. Speaks to stress and overwhelm directly, then offers a calm, concrete next step.
